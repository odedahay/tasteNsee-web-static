My Journey

From Nursing to Cake Design

As a registered nurse, I’ve always believed that caring for people goes beyond healthcare.

Baking became my creative outlet—a way to bring comfort, joy, and celebration into people’s lives.

Over the years, that passion evolved into tasteNsee, where every cake is handcrafted with premium ingredients, thoughtful design, and attention to every detail.

Today I’m grateful to create cakes for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, and celebrations of every kind while also offering inclusive workshops where children of all abilities can learn, create, and belong.
