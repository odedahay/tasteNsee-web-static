About this Workshop

Creative Edible Arts Workshops

These hands-on workshops invite young makers to explore colour, texture, and imagination through edible art. Each child creates a one-of-a-kind cake or dessert while learning approachable decorating techniques with buttercream, fondant, and playful finishing details.

Every session is fun, engaging, and inclusive for kids of all abilities, with extra care for children who benefit from a patient pace or additional support. Small groups and step-by-step guidance help every child feel confident, included, and proud of the edible artwork they take home.

Workshop Schedule

A rough guide to how the 2 hours flows

  1. 10:00Welcome & aprons on — meet your fellow bakers!
  2. 10:15Meet the materials — colour, texture, tools, and safe techniques
  3. 10:35Build your edible canvas with guided, step-by-step decorating
  4. 11:00Create buttercream, fondant, and edible-art details
  5. 11:35Add personal finishing touches and decorate your presentation board
  6. 11:50Celebrate, share photos, and take your creation home!

What You'll Learn

  • Confident use of child-friendly decorating tools
  • Buttercream piping and simple fondant details
  • Colour mixing, texture, and edible decoration
  • Basic composition and creative planning
  • Creative confidence, patience, and self-expression

What's Included

  • All materials and ingredients provided
  • A personal edible-art creation to take home
  • Patient, step-by-step guided instruction
  • Apron provided for the class
  • Take-home recipe card and tip sheet
  • Celebration and photo moment at the end

Good to Know

  • Suitable for ages 6–14
  • All abilities and experience levels are welcome
  • Comfortable clothing recommended
  • Please note allergies or support needs during registration
