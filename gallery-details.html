About this cake

Ivory Rose Tier

A signature tasteNsee design, the Ivory Rose Tier has graced dozens of Calgary weddings. Every petal is hand-pressed, every tier perfectly level — an edible centrepiece that photographs as beautifully as it tastes.

Available in two to four tiers, this cake scales gracefully from an intimate dinner for forty to a ballroom reception for over one hundred. The floral arrangement is fully custom — choose from a cascade of roses, a symmetrical crest, or a loose garden cluster that wraps around the base.

Both fondant and buttercream finishes are available. Fondant gives the sharpest edges and the cleanest canvas for sugar flowers; a textured buttercream finish adds warmth and a hand-crafted feeling that photographs particularly well in golden light.
